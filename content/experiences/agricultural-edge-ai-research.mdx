## Highlights

- Audited a 14,154-image wheat disease benchmark via MD5+pHash deduplication, eliminating 11.6% cross-split leakage.
- Investigated quantization behavior across MobileNetV3-L, ResNet50, and ConvNeXt-Tiny architectures.
- Restored MobileNetV3 INT8 accuracy from 31.0% to 82.5% via entropy-calibrated TensorRT.
- Engineered HardSwish/LayerNorm deployment patches for stable FP16/INT8 inference on Jetson Nano.
- Proposed the Deployment Efficiency Score (DES) metric, achieving 54.5 FPS real-time edge inference on embedded hardware.
